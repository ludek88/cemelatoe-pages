Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 27 June 2026

Cemelatoe Automation is an operator-owned tool for managing creator social media workflows. If you want data associated with your interaction with this app deleted, you can request deletion by email.

How To Request Deletion

Send an email to ludek@kozacki.pl with the subject line:

Data deletion request - Cemelatoe Automation

Please include:

  • Your Facebook or Instagram username, if applicable.
  • A short description of the data you want deleted.
  • A contact email where we can confirm completion.

What Will Be Deleted

We will delete locally stored operational records associated with the request where reasonably identifiable, such as draft reply records, publishing logs, and cached engagement data.

Platform Data

Content, comments, messages, or account information stored directly by Meta, Facebook, Instagram, or other platforms must be managed through those platforms' own privacy and account tools.

Timing

We will review deletion requests and respond within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days.
