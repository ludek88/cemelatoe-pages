Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 June 2026

Camela Toe Automation is an operator-owned automation tool used to manage creator social media publishing, media review, and engagement workflows for accounts controlled by the app owner.

Information We Access

When connected to Meta, Facebook, or Instagram APIs, the app may access account identifiers, page identifiers, Instagram business account identifiers, media identifiers, captions, public comments, comment IDs, basic profile/account information, and publishing status required to operate the connected account.

How We Use Information

The app uses this information only to publish approved media, inspect publishing status, read comments on owned media, and draft or send approved replies on behalf of the account owner.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except service providers required to operate hosting, logging, storage, or official platform API integrations.

Storage And Security

Access tokens and operational data are stored in private operator-controlled environments. Secrets are not committed to public source control. Access is limited to the account operator and authorized automation services.

Data Retention

Operational records are retained only as long as needed for debugging, audit, publishing history, and account operations. The operator may delete stored local records at any time.

User Data Deletion

To request deletion of data associated with this app, follow the instructions on the Data Deletion Instructions page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: contact@camelatoe.com
